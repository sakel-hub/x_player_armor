### Textures
All armor and shield texture sheets, inventory sprites, UI textures, and VFX particle sheets (CC-BY 4.0):
- Unified 64x32 character armor sheets: `textures/x_player_armor_<material>.png` (admin, bronze, cactus, crystal, diamond, gold, mithril, nether, steel, wood)
- Dedicated 2D pixel-art inventory sprites: `textures/x_player_armor_inv_*.png`
- Enhanced shields: `textures/x_player_armor_shield_enhanced_cactus.png`, `textures/x_player_armor_shield_enhanced_wood.png`
- Stand, UI, and character skin textures: `textures/x_player_armor_stand_*.png`, `textures/x_player_armor_icon.png`, `textures/x_player_armor_inventory_plus.png`, `textures/x_player_armor_character.png`
- Combat VFX particle sheet: `textures/x_player_armor_particles.png` (8 frames: healing sparkles, wards, shield deflection, and armor shatter shards) designed by SaKeL under CC-BY 4.0 / CC0 1.0.
Authored, redesigned, and packaged for Luanti by SaKeL under CC-BY 4.0.
